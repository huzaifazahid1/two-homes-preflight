PARENTING AGREEMENT: CHILD EXPENSES

This Parenting Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on March 3, 2025, between Alex Rivera ("Parent A") and Sara Rivera ("Parent B"), together "the Parents".

1. The Child

1.1 The Parents have one child, Mia Rivera, born in 2016 ("the Child").

1.2 This Agreement sets out how the Parents share the Child's expenses. It does not change any custody or parenting-time order.

2. Definitions

2.1 "Purchasing Parent" means the parent who pays a vendor for an expense for the Child.

2.2 "Other Parent" means the parent who did not pay the vendor.

2.3 "Written approval" includes approval given by email, by text message, or through an expense-sharing application.

3. General principles

3.1 The Parents will cooperate in good faith and keep each other informed about significant expenses for the Child.

3.2 Each Parent pays the Child's ordinary day-to-day costs, such as food, toiletries and household items, while the Child is in that Parent's care. These costs are not shared.

3.3 The Parents will communicate about shared expenses in writing so that there is a clear record.

4. Receipts and reimbursement

4.1 The Purchasing Parent shall give the Other Parent a copy of the receipt within thirty (30) days of the purchase.

4.2 The Other Parent shall reimburse the Purchasing Parent for their share within thirty (30) days of receiving the receipt.

4.3 A Parent who does not provide a receipt on time may still ask for reimbursement, but the Other Parent may decline a late request.

5. Medical, dental and vision expenses

5.1 Uninsured medical, dental and vision expenses for the Child shall be shared equally (50/50) by the Parents.

5.2 Routine check-ups, prescriptions and dental cleanings do not require prior approval.

5.3 Either Parent may consent to emergency treatment for the Child without the Other Parent's approval, and the cost is shared under clause 5.1.

5.4 Health insurance premiums remain the responsibility of Parent A as set out in the separate support order and are not shared under this Agreement.

6. Extracurricular activities

6.1 Extracurricular activity costs, including sports, camps, music and art lessons, are shared 60/40, with the Purchasing Parent paying sixty percent (60%) and the Other Parent reimbursing forty percent (40%), and any single extracurricular expense above $200 requires the Other Parent's written approval before the purchase.

6.2 The Parents will not withhold approval without a good reason for activities the Child already attends.

6.3 Equipment and uniforms needed for an approved activity are part of that activity's cost.

7. School and education

7.1 School supplies, textbooks and school field trip fees shall be shared equally (50/50) by the Parents.

7.2 Private school tuition is not covered by this Agreement and will be the subject of a separate written decision.

8. Clothing

8.1 The Parents shall share reasonable clothing expenses for the Child equally.

8.2 School uniforms are treated as clothing under clause 8.1.

10. Disagreements

10.1 If the Parents disagree about a shared expense, they will first try to resolve it in writing within fourteen (14) days.
